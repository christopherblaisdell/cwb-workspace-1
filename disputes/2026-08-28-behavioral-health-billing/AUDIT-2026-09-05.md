# Case File Audit and Rationalization — 2026-09-05

**Status: authoritative.** This document supersedes the priority ordering in every other
file in this folder. Where any other document conflicts with this one, this one governs
until the conflict is fixed at source.

Every file in the folder was re-read on 2026-09-05 and checked for four things:

1. **Factual integrity** — is each assertion witnessed, reported, inferred, or unverified?
2. **Legal accuracy** — is every citation real, correctly applied, and current?
3. **Internal consistency** — do the files contradict each other?
4. **Send-readiness** — can any outbound document actually go out today?

The answer to (4) is **no**, and the reason matters.

---

## The one-paragraph verdict

The research quality in this folder is high. The refusal to reach for the Establishment
Clause, ACA § 1557, and Title II — when it would have been easy to cite them — is genuinely
disciplined lawyering-adjacent work, and the citations are accurate far more often than not.
But the folder has a **fact-laundering problem**: something reported by a third party in one
file becomes "confirmed" in the next, and by the third file it is in a letter the patient
signs. Roughly **a dozen assertions that appear in signed outbound correspondence are not
supported by anything in the file.** That is not a drafting nit. Each one is a prepared
impeachment question, and the weakest of them will be used to discredit the strongest claims.

The fix is not to soften the case. It is to **say only what can be proven, and let the
documents the facility itself produces supply the rest.**

### 1.3 The three claims that must stop being asserted as fact

These appear in outbound letters, in draft correspondence to third parties, and in at least
one document signed under penalty of perjury. Each is currently unsupported.

1. 🔴 **"I did not sign the intake paperwork — facility staff signed my name."**
   Appears flatly in `13`, `14`, `15`, `20`, `21`, `24`, and in a **draft letter to an outside
   organisation**. But `16` concedes the actual evidentiary position is nearer to *"I have no
   memory of signing"*, and resolving it will require a questioned-documents examiner and
   exemplars that have not been collected.
   → **Required form everywhere:** *"I have no recollection of signing the admission or
   financial documents, and I do not believe the signatures on them are mine. I have asked
   the facility to produce them."* Nothing stronger until an examiner has looked.

2. 🔴 **"They faxed my records to an unrelated law office."**
   Christopher did not receive the call, did not see the fax, does not know the firm's name,
   the date, or the page count. **The entire factual predicate is his wife's account of one
   phone call.** `14` calls its own facts "Confirmed" in a table where four of eight rows say
   *unknown*. The file also presents a false binary — "they knew and didn't notify" vs. "they
   don't know" — that omits the possibility that **it was not Remedy's fax at all**.
   → **Required form:** *"I have been informed by my wife that a law office with no
   relationship to us contacted her regarding my treatment records."* The fax header is what
   converts this to fact. Get it first.

3. 🔴 **"I attempted to reach your office by telephone on multiple occasions without success."**
   This sits in letter `01`, which the patient signs. **The communication log contains zero
   logged calls**, and the intake form is still asking the wife to reconstruct them from
   memory. This is the easiest sentence in the entire file to impeach, and it is attached to
   the most important letter.
   → **Either** reconstruct the calls from phone records **first**, **or** delete the sentence.
   It adds nothing. The 30-day HIPAA clock does not depend on it.

### 1.4 Defamation exposure — the "Josh D" problem

`24` names an identifiable individual and builds an inferential accusation that he generated
false documentation supporting fraudulent psychotherapy billing, and asserts he is "the same
unlicensed staff member" — in a file that elsewhere lists his licence status as **unverified**.
The file's own caution against naming him is correct and is then breached by the file's own
analysis.

**Rule adopted:** in any document that could leave this folder, individual staff are referred
to **by role and by the date and time observed**, never by name. Investigators establish who
was on shift. That is their job, they are good at it, and it costs nothing to let them do it.

The same rule kills the **review-manipulation insinuation** in `22` — speculating that
five-star reviews were solicited from patients "still in treatment and dependent on staff,"
inferred from nothing but a bimodal star distribution. Unsupported, unnecessary, and it
sounds actionable. **Cut it.**

### The meta-pattern, stated once

Across `14`, `15`, `18`, `19`, and `20` the same structure recurs: a fact appears in bold as
**"Confirmed"** or **"✅ ANSWERED"** near the top of a file, and the **same file's** open-question
list at the bottom asks for that identical fact. `15` asserts that individuals were
recognizable on the monitors and then says the question that matters most is whether
individuals were recognizable on the monitors.

The likely cause is iterative drafting where answered sections were added without pruning the
questions they answered. Whatever the cause, **the file set does not currently distinguish
what is known from what is hoped**, and the summary tables in `19`, `23`, and `24` strip the
hedges the body text carefully applied.

`FACT-REGISTER.md` exists to end this. Every material assertion now has exactly one home and
exactly one evidence tier.

## Part 4 — Evidence integrity

**The `curl` capture is well above amateur standard. The rendered-testimonial capture — which
holds the most probative content — is not.**

What is done well, and should be said out loud: a complete SHA-256 manifest over all 48 files,
verified as internally accurate; **HTTP response headers preserved separately** for all 16
pages, which most self-collected web evidence omits entirely and which independently
corroborate retrieval time; unmodified response bodies with text extraction kept as a separate
derived artifact; full provenance metadata; and an honest self-assessment that correctly ranks
*facility's own records in discovery > independent archive > self-collected capture*. That
caveat's presence **increases** the capture's credibility.

Four gaps, in priority order:

1. 🔴 **The hashes have no independent time anchor.** SHA-256 proves nothing changed *since the
   sums file was written* — and the sums file was written by the same operator, on the same
   machine, at the same moment, and can be regenerated at will. **Nothing ties it to
   2026-08-28.**
   → **Fixed:** `.gitignore` now tracks the website capture. It is the facility's **public
   marketing site** — it contains no PHI and never did, so the blanket `evidence/*` exclusion
   was over-broad. Committing it puts the hashes in a git history. Sign the commit, and
   additionally send `SHA256SUMS.txt` to yourself through an external mail provider or run
   OpenTimestamps over it. Both are free and take minutes.

2. 🔴 **`RENDERED-TESTIMONIALS.md` has no artifact, no hash, and an under-specified method.**
   The Bible-study testimonial is arguably the most valuable single piece of marketing
   evidence in the case, and **it exists only as prose typed into a Markdown file.** No DOM
   dump, no PDF, no screenshot, nothing in `SHA256SUMS.txt`, no browser or version recorded.
   The file acknowledges the gap and says to produce a browser PDF — that has not been done.
   Until it is, this is **transcription, not capture**, and it is impeachable as such.

3. 🟠 **Twelve of sixteen Wayback saves are unverified**, and the four confirmed ones are
   `conditions`, `verify-my-insurance`, `privacy-policy`, and `hipaa-privacy-policy` — **none
   of which contains the testimonials, the CBT claim, or the catered-meals language.** The
   independent corroboration covers precisely the pages that do not matter.
   → Highest-leverage remaining evidence task: confirm or re-submit `/`,
   `/treatments/individual-therapy/`, and `/about/our-facility/`.

4. 🟠 **The "zero matches" negative finding has no preserved method or output**, and the term
   list is too narrow to support its conclusion. It omits *spirituality, the steps, sponsor,
   fellowship, AA, NA, God, higher power as we understand, surrender, recovery meeting.*
   A negative finding is only as good as its search. Re-run it with a broad term set, save
   the command and the output, and hash both.

Also unresolved: **"Josh D appears elsewhere as Activities Director" is unsourced to any
captured artifact**, and `24` builds a fraud narrative on that role. Capture and hash the
source, or drop the inference.

## Part 6 — Standing rules

**Never assert:**
- That the signatures are forged. Say you have no recollection of signing and do not believe
  they are yours.
- That the facility faxed the records. Say your wife was contacted by a law office with no
  relationship to you.
- That you called repeatedly, unless the phone records are in hand.
- That the session was not psychotherapy. Say the conduct is inconsistent with what CPT 90853
  describes, and that the codes billed will resolve it.
- Anything about a named individual staff member. Role, date, time.

**Always preserve:**
- The distinction between what was **seen** and what was **told**. This is `10`'s best
  guidance and it must survive into every other document.
- The verbatim quotes, unaltered: *"It looks like you are having your own little group here.
  Sign this paper."* and *"SOME of us will be praying."* If certainty about exact wording is
  less than total, write *"words to the effect of."*
- The instruction never to name the assault victims, never to contact other patients, never to
  return to the facility, and never to post publicly. Those instructions are correct and are
  the best risk management in the folder.

**One more thing worth saying plainly.** The honest version of this case is stronger than the
maximal version. A regulator who can verify three concrete things in thirty seconds will
investigate the rest. A regulator who catches one overstatement will discount everything.
`15` says this about itself and then does not follow its own rule. Follow it.
