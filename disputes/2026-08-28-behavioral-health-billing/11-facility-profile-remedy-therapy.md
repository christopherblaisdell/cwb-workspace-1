From their **Individual Therapy** section, verbatim:

> options, including cognitive-behavioral therapy**, to provide healing for our patients."

You asked for CBT or a secular alternative and were told none was available. They
advertise CBT on their homepage. That is the FDUTPA claim in one sentence.

The `recovered.org` listing independently reports their interventions as **"anger
management, cognitive behavioral therapy, contingency management, motivational
interviewing and matrix model"** — all secular, evidence-based modalities. None of them
is 12-step.

### 2. They advertise individualized, clinical care

> "We strive to **tailor the treatment options to the individual's challenges**."

> Group Therapy: "a supportive and collaborative environment where individuals can
> connect with others... **gain insight, and develop coping skills**."

### 3. ⭐ Nothing on their website discloses that the program is 12-step or faith-based

I found no disclosure anywhere on the treatments pages that programming is twelve-step
based or religious in content. Under FDUTPA, a **material omission** is actionable the

### ⭐ 6. The "Our Team" page names nobody — and that is a finding

I checked `remedytherapybehavioralhealth.com/about/our-team/` in a full browser, scrolled
to the bottom to trigger lazy loading, and extracted the rendered text.

**There are no staff names on it. None.** No clinical director, no medical director, no
therapists. No credentials, no licenses, no photographs with titles. The entire page is

For a **Joint Commission–accredited** residential treatment facility, that is unusual.
Legitimate programs name their medical director and clinical leadership with credentials,

## Why Joint Commission accreditation matters here

If TJC accreditation is confirmed, it's one of your strongest levers:

- TJC standards require respect for patients' **cultural, spiritual, and personal
  values**, **individualized** care planning, and an accessible **grievance process**.
- TJC investigates patient complaints and can require corrective action; accreditation is
  tied to reimbursement, so facilities take TJC complaints far more seriously than
  patient letters.
- File at `jointcommission.org` → "Report a Patient Safety Event."
- Include: the group exclusion incident, the denial of a secular alternative, the
  facility's own published testimonial showing accommodation of a Christian patient, the
  unresponsiveness to your records requests, and the signature issue (see
  [12](12-forged-signatures.md)).
