## 2. ⭐⭐⭐ Send the Chapter 119 public records requests

Text in [17-witnessed-patient-fall.md](17-witnessed-patient-fall.md). To **Martin County
Fire Rescue** and **Martin County 911 / dispatch**.

**Highest-value action available to you**, because of what it unlocks:

```
   COUNTY EMS / 911 RECORDS          THE FACILITY'S OWN BILLING
   ─────────────────────────         ──────────────────────────
   [DATE], approx [TIME]             [SAME DATE], approx [SAME TIME]
   Emergency response, unconscious   Group psychotherapy, CPT 90853
   patient, trauma transport         Roster signed by residents
```

**Both cannot be true — and neither document comes from you.**

You don't need to remember the date. The EMS record supplies it. Then match it to the
roster.

Ask for: EMS run reports, CAD logs, 911 recordings, trauma alert records, and the **full
five-year fire marshal inspection history**.

## 3. Report the stairway as an ongoing hazard

**Martin County / City of Stuart Fire Marshal** and **Building Department**.

Lead with: *"To my knowledge the stairway still has no continuous handrail and detox
patients may still be housed upstairs. I believe patients are at risk today."*

Ongoing danger gets someone dispatched. Historical complaints get filed.

## 4. Write your incident statement

[10-incident-statement.md](10-incident-statement.md). Florida allows an unsworn declaration
— no notary needed.

**The one rule that matters: separate what you SAW from what you were TOLD.** You saw the
fall and heard the gurgling. You were *told* about the helicopter.

Write the anchor sequence exactly: where you were sitting, the fall, the sounds, the
ambulance, then the paper and what was said. And preserve this verbatim:

> *"It looks like you are having your own little group here. Sign this paper."*

## 5. Call Disability Rights Florida — **1-800-342-0823**

Free. Federally funded. Behavioral health patient rights is literally their mandate, and
they can inspect facilities.

**If you make one call this week, make it this one.**

## 6. ⚠️ One call to a qui tam attorney before filing anything about the rosters

A **sequencing** issue, not a delay.

If the facility bills **TRICARE, Medicare, or Medicaid** for any patient, the false rosters
may support a federal False Claims Act case — which has a **first-to-file rule** and a
**public disclosure bar**. Describing them in a public complaint could complicate it.

*(A directory lists them as accepting military insurance. TRICARE is federal.)*

**One free consultation resolves it.** Everything else below files now.
