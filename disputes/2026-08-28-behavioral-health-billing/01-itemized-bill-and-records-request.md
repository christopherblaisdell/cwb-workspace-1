# 01 — Itemized Bill Request + HIPAA Right of Access Records Request

> **Send by:** Certified Mail w/ Return Receipt (billing **and** medical records
> addresses) **+** fax to both departments **+** email to all known addresses **+**
> patient portal message. Same day. See [00-send-checklist.md](00-send-checklist.md).
>
> **This is your first written contact.** It does four things at once: demands the
> itemized bill, invokes the federal 30-day HIPAA records deadline, demands an accounting
> of disclosures plus every authorization form bearing your signature, and formally
> disputes the balance so it is contested from day one.
>
> ⛔ **Send it exactly as written. Do NOT mention that the intake signatures are forged**
> — see [12-forged-signatures.md](12-forged-signatures.md). The requests below are
> neutral on their face and will produce the documents before they know why you want
> them.
>
> **Attach:** copy of photo ID; copy of insurance card (front/back); copy of the bill.

To Whom It May Concern:

I am the patient identified above. I have attempted to reach your office by

I am therefore submitting this request in writing.

This letter constitutes (1) a request for a fully itemized statement of account,
(2) a formal request for access to my complete medical record under the HIPAA
Privacy Rule, and (3) a formal dispute of the alleged balance of $4,000.00.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART ONE — REQUEST FOR FULLY ITEMIZED STATEMENT
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

Please provide a complete, line-item itemized statement for the above account
covering the entire episode of care. The summary statement I received is not
sufficient to verify the charges. The itemization must include, for each and
every charge:

  1.  Date of service for each individual line item.
  2.  CPT / HCPCS procedure code and full narrative description.
  3.  Revenue codes, and the corresponding level of care billed for each date
      (e.g., residential treatment, partial hospitalization, intensive
      outpatient, detoxification, room and board).
  4.  Per-diem rate charged for each date of service.
  5.  Units billed and the rendering provider's name, credential, and NPI for
      each line item.
  6.  All ancillary charges itemized separately, including laboratory and
      toxicology/urine drug screening (with the specific test panels and codes),
      pharmacy and medication administration, physician and mid-level provider
      encounters, psychiatric evaluations, individual therapy, group therapy,
      family therapy, and case management.
  7.  A complete payment and adjustment ledger showing: gross charges,
      contractual adjustments, amounts billed to [INSURER NAME], amounts paid by
      [INSURER NAME], any amounts paid by me, any write-offs, and the resulting
      balance.
  8.  Copies of the UB-04 and/or CMS-1500 claim forms as actually submitted to
      [INSURER NAME], including all claim revisions, corrected claims, and
      resubmissions.
  9.  Copies of all Explanation of Benefits and remittance advice documents your
      office received from [INSURER NAME] for this account.
 10.  The specific written basis on which your office calculated my patient
      responsibility to be $4,000.00, identifying whether this amount is
      characterized as a deductible, copayment, coinsurance, non-covered
      service, or out-of-network balance, and identifying the contractual or
      plan provision relied upon.
 11.  Copies of every financial responsibility agreement, admission consent, or
      payment authorization bearing my signature that your office relies upon to
      assert this debt.
 12.  A copy of the Good Faith Estimate provided to me prior to or at admission,
      together with proof of its delivery.
 13.  A copy of your facility's financial assistance / charity care policy and
      application, and your sliding-scale and hardship discount policies.
 14.  For every group session billed to this account: the name of the group, the
      name and professional license or credential of the individual who
      facilitated it, and the CPT/HCPCS code billed for it.
 15.  Copies of the facility's daily and weekly program schedules in effect
      during my dates of service.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART TWO — REQUEST FOR ACCESS TO MEDICAL RECORDS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

Pursuant to my right of access under the HIPAA Privacy Rule, 45 C.F.R.
§ 164.524, and under applicable [YOUR STATE] law, I request a complete copy of
my designated record set for the above dates of service, including but not
limited to:

  a.  Admission assessment, biopsychosocial assessment, and initial psychiatric
      evaluation.
  b.  The individualized treatment plan and every treatment plan review, update,
      and revision, with dates and signatures.
  c.  All physician, psychiatrist, nurse practitioner, and physician assistant
      progress notes and encounter notes, with dates and times.
  d.  All nursing notes, shift notes, observation and safety-check logs, and
      medication administration records (MAR).
  e.  All individual therapy notes and all group therapy notes, including group
      attendance rosters and sign-in sheets showing which groups I attended, the
      dates, times, duration, and the name and credential of the facilitator.
  f.  Documentation of all family sessions, case management contacts, and
      discharge planning activity.
  g.  All laboratory and toxicology results and the corresponding orders.
  h.  All incident reports, restraint/seclusion records, and grievance records
      involving me.
  i.  The discharge summary, discharge instructions, and aftercare plan.
  j.  All utilization review and medical necessity documentation, and all
      correspondence between your facility and [INSURER NAME] concerning
      authorization, concurrent review, level of care, or medical necessity for
      my admission.
  k.  The staffing and census records for my unit for my dates of service, to
      the extent they form part of the record of my care.
  l.  All documentation of any request I made for an alternative treatment
      modality, and of the facility's response.
  m.  Copies of the facility's written policies on patient rights, religious
      accommodation and non-discrimination, treatment planning, and the patient
      grievance process, as in effect during my admission.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART TWO-A — ACCOUNTING OF DISCLOSURES AND AUTHORIZATION FORMS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

Separately from the request for access above, and pursuant to 45 C.F.R.
§ 164.528, I request an ACCOUNTING OF DISCLOSURES of my protected health
information. Please provide a written accounting of each disclosure made by
your facility, including for each disclosure:

  i.    the date of the disclosure;
  ii.   the name and address of the entity or person who received the
        information;
  iii.  a brief description of the protected health information disclosed; and
  iv.   a brief statement of the purpose of the disclosure, or a copy of the
        written request or authorization relied upon.

In addition, please provide:

  v.    copies of every written authorization, consent to disclosure, or
        release of information form bearing my name or signature that your
        facility has relied upon to disclose any of my records to any party,
        including the date each was purportedly executed and the identity of
        any witness or staff member who signed it; and
  vi.   copies of your facility's written policies and procedures governing
        disclosure of patient records, including those adopted to comply with
        42 C.F.R. Part 2.

My records are records of a substance use disorder treatment program and are
therefore subject to the confidentiality protections of 42 C.F.R. Part 2 in
addition to the HIPAA Privacy Rule. Please note that 42 C.F.R. Part 2 governs
disclosure of my records to third parties; it does not limit my own right of
access to my records, and it may not be relied upon as a basis to delay or deny
this request.

I request these records in electronic form (searchable PDF) delivered to
[YOUR EMAIL] or on encrypted media mailed to my address above, whichever your
office prefers. I understand you may charge a reasonable, cost-based fee for
copies as permitted under 45 C.F.R. § 164.524(c)(4); please advise me of that
fee in writing in advance. I do not authorize any charge for search, retrieval,
or administrative time, which is not permitted under that provision.

Please note that 45 C.F.R. § 164.524(b)(2) requires you to act on this request
no later than THIRTY (30) CALENDAR DAYS from receipt. If you require the single
permitted extension, you must notify me in writing within that 30-day period
stating the reason and the date by which you will comply. Absent such written
notice, I will treat any response after [DATE 30 DAYS FROM SENDING] as a denial
of access.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART THREE — FORMAL DISPUTE OF THE ALLEGED BALANCE
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

I formally and in good faith dispute the alleged balance of $4,000.00 in its
entirety. My dispute is based on, among other grounds, the following:

  •  The programming delivered during this admission consisted substantially of
     twelve-step mutual-help meetings. Twelve-step meetings are peer support
     groups available without charge in the community and are not clinical
     services. I dispute that clinical service rates were properly billed for
     those hours.
  •  I am an atheist. I requested a secular, evidence-based treatment
     alternative. None was offered or provided, and my treatment plan was not
     individualized to account for this.
  •  I was excluded from at least one treatment group on the basis of my
     religious beliefs, while being billed for the treatment program of which
     that group was a part.
  •  The services actually rendered to me during this admission did not
     correspond to the services and level of care I believe have been billed.
  •  I have not been provided with sufficient documentation to verify that the
     charges asserted are accurate or that the patient-responsibility
     calculation is correct.

I reserve the right to supplement and particularize this dispute upon receipt of
the itemized statement and medical records requested above. Nothing in this
letter is an acknowledgment of the validity of any portion of the alleged debt.

Accordingly, I request that you:

  1.  Place this account on administrative hold pending resolution of this
      dispute.
  2.  NOT refer, sell, assign, or otherwise transfer this account to any
      collection agency or debt buyer while this dispute is pending.
  3.  NOT report this account, or any portion of it, to any consumer reporting
      agency while this dispute is pending. If this account has already been
      reported, report it as disputed as required by the Fair Credit Reporting
      Act, 15 U.S.C. § 1681s-2.
  4.  Direct all further communication regarding this account to me IN WRITING
      at the postal and email addresses above. Given the repeated failure of
      telephone contact, written communication is necessary to maintain an
      accurate record.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
RESPONSE REQUESTED
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

Please provide the itemized statement requested in Part One within FOURTEEN (14)
calendar days, and the records requested in Part Two within the thirty (30)
calendar days required by federal law.

If I do not receive a substantive written response, I intend to pursue this
matter through the appropriate external channels, which may include a complaint
to the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, Office for Civil Rights
regarding denial of records access; a complaint to [YOUR STATE] licensing
authority for behavioral health facilities; a complaint to your accrediting
organization; and a complaint to the [YOUR STATE] Attorney General's consumer
protection division.

I would prefer to resolve this directly and cooperatively with your office, and
I remain available to do so in writing.

Please confirm receipt of this letter in writing.

## Short version for email / patient portal

Use this as the body of the email and portal message, with the full letter attached
as a PDF. A short message is more likely to actually be read.

```
Subject: WRITTEN REQUEST — Itemized Bill & Medical Records — [YOUR NAME], Acct [ACCOUNT NUMBER]

To Billing, Medical Records, and the Patient Advocate:

I have called your office repeatedly and been unable to reach anyone, so I am
submitting this in writing.

Attached is my formal written request for (1) a fully itemized statement for
account [ACCOUNT NUMBER], dates of service [ADMIT] to [DISCHARGE], and (2) a
complete copy of my medical record under my HIPAA right of access, 45 C.F.R.
§ 164.524.

I also formally dispute the alleged balance of $4,000.00 in its entirety and
request that the account be placed on hold and not referred to collections or
reported to any credit bureau while the dispute is pending.

This same request has been sent today by certified mail (tracking
[TRACKING NUMBER]) and by fax to [FAX NUMBER].

Federal law requires you to act on the records request within 30 calendar days
of receipt. Please confirm receipt of this message in writing.

[YOUR FULL LEGAL NAME]
DOB [DOB] | Account [ACCOUNT NUMBER] | MRN [MRN]
[YOUR PHONE] | [YOUR EMAIL]
```
