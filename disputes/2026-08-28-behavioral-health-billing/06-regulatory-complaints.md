# 06 — Regulatory Complaints

> **File all of these in the same week.** They are free, they run in parallel, and each
> one independently forces a written response from the facility. A facility that
> ignores a patient will not ignore its licensing board or its accreditor.
>
> Use the shared narrative below for every complaint — write it once, reuse it.

---

## Where to file

| Body | What they act on | How to file |
|---|---|---|
| **HHS Office for Civil Rights** | Denial of HIPAA right of access (missed 30-day records deadline). Strongest and most direct lever. | `ocrportal.hhs.gov/ocr/cp` — must file within 180 days of the violation |
| **[YOUR STATE] behavioral health licensing agency** | Quality of care, treatment plan failures, staffing, patient rights. Usually the Dept. of Health, Dept. of Behavioral Health/Human Services, or a substance use licensing division. | Search "[YOUR STATE] file complaint behavioral health facility" |
| **The Joint Commission** | Accredited facilities. Investigates quality/safety and can put accreditation at risk. | `jointcommission.org` → Report a Patient Safety Event. Find who accredits them at `qualitycheck.org` |
| **CARF International** | If CARF-accredited instead. | `carf.org` → file a complaint |
| **[YOUR STATE] Attorney General, Consumer Protection** | Deceptive billing, unresponsiveness, billing for services not rendered. | State AG website consumer complaint form |
| **[YOUR STATE] Department of Insurance** | Insurer's handling of the claim; parity violations. | State DOI consumer complaint form |
| **U.S. DOL / EBSA** | If employer self-funded plan — plan's failure to follow claims procedures. | 1-866-444-3272 or `askebsa.dol.gov` |
| **Consumer Financial Protection Bureau** | Any collection or credit-reporting activity on a disputed medical debt. | `consumerfinance.gov/complaint` |
| **State Medicaid Program Integrity / HHS-OIG** | Only if Medicaid/Medicare paid any portion AND you find services billed but not rendered. | `oig.hhs.gov/fraud/report-fraud` |
| **NAMI / state protection & advocacy agency** | Free advocacy support for behavioral health patients. Every state has a federally funded P&A agency. | Search "[YOUR STATE] protection and advocacy mental health" |

## Shared complaint narrative (reuse for each filing)

Keep it factual and chronological. Regulators respond to dates and documents, not
adjectives. Attach the correspondence and the delivery receipts — that is what makes
this credible.

```
COMPLAINANT
    Name:        [YOUR FULL LEGAL NAME]
    Address:     [YOUR ADDRESS]
    Phone/Email: [PHONE] / [EMAIL]
    Relationship to patient: Self

FACILITY COMPLAINED OF
    Name:     [FACILITY LEGAL NAME]
    Address:  [FACILITY ADDRESS]
    License #: [IF KNOWN]
    Corporate parent: [IF KNOWN]
    Type:     Residential behavioral health treatment facility

PATIENT / EPISODE
    Dates of service: [ADMIT DATE] through [DISCHARGE DATE]
    Account number:   [ACCOUNT NUMBER]
    Amount in dispute: $4,000.00

SUMMARY OF COMPLAINT

I was a resident at [FACILITY] from [ADMIT DATE] to [DISCHARGE DATE]. I am
filing this complaint on two grounds: (1) the treatment I received was
materially deficient and did not conform to the treatment plan presented to me,
and (2) the facility has refused to respond to repeated written requests for an
itemized bill and for my own medical records, while continuing to assert that I
owe $4,000.00.

I.  QUALITY OF CARE

    [Write factually and chronologically. Use dates. Stick to what happened.
     Useful things to address if true:

     - Services promised at admission vs. services actually delivered
       (frequency of individual therapy, psychiatric contact, group programming).
     - Whether an individualized treatment plan was created, reviewed, and
       followed.
     - Delays in medication management or psychiatric evaluation.
     - Staffing levels; whether groups were run by credentialed clinicians.
     - Whether concerns were raised to staff, to whom, on what date, and what
       response was given.
     - Whether a grievance process was explained or made available to you.
     - Discharge planning and aftercare.

     Example of the register to aim for:
     "At admission on [DATE] I was told I would receive individual therapy twice
     weekly. I received [N] individual sessions during a [N]-day admission. On
     [DATE] I raised this with [NAME/ROLE] and was told [RESPONSE]. No change
     followed."]

II. FAILURE TO PRODUCE RECORDS AND ITEMIZED BILLING

    2026-08-28  I sent a written request for an itemized statement and for my
                complete medical record under 45 C.F.R. § 164.524, together with
                a formal dispute of the $4,000.00 balance. Sent by certified mail
                (USPS tracking [NUMBER]), fax, email, and patient portal.
    [DATE]      Delivered; return receipt signed by [NAME].
    [DATE]      Second written request sent by the same methods (tracking
                [NUMBER]); delivered [DATE].
    [DATE]      Final written demand sent (tracking [NUMBER]); delivered [DATE].
    [DATES]     [N] telephone calls placed. None answered; none returned.

    As of [TODAY'S DATE], [N] days after the facility received my first request,
    I have received [no response / an incomplete response consisting of ___].

    The facility's 30-day deadline under 45 C.F.R. § 164.524(b)(2) expired on
    [DEADLINE DATE]. The facility provided no written extension notice.

III. HARM

    I am being asked to pay $4,000.00 for care I believe was inadequate, and I
    am being denied the documents I need to verify or contest the charge. I
    face potential collection activity and credit reporting on a debt the
    facility will not substantiate.

IV. RELIEF REQUESTED

    1.  That the facility be required to produce my complete medical record and
        a fully itemized statement.
    2.  Investigation of whether services billed were actually rendered.
    3.  Investigation of the quality of care and treatment plan compliance
        during my admission.
    4.  Correction of the account and cessation of collection activity pending
        resolution.

DOCUMENTATION ATTACHED

    - Correspondence dated 2026-08-28, [DATE], [DATE]
    - USPS certified mail receipts and return receipts
    - Fax transmission confirmations
    - Email and patient portal screenshots with timestamps
    - Statement from [FACILITY] dated [DATE]
    - Explanation of Benefits from [INSURER NAME] dated [DATE]
    - Telephone contact log

I declare that the foregoing is true and correct to the best of my knowledge.

_______________________________          [DATE]
[YOUR FULL LEGAL NAME]
```

## Notifying the facility that complaints have been filed

Send this short letter to the facility after filing. It frequently produces the
response that three prior letters did not.

```
[DATE]

[FACILITY LEGAL NAME]
Attn: Administrator; Compliance Officer
[ADDRESS]

RE:  Notice of Regulatory Complaints Filed — [YOUR NAME],
     Account [ACCOUNT NUMBER], DOS [DATES]

To the Administrator and Compliance Officer:

Having received no substantive response to my written correspondence of
2026-08-28, [DATE], and [DATE], I have today filed complaints with the
following:

  •  U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, Office for Civil Rights
     (Complaint No. [NUMBER])
  •  [STATE LICENSING AGENCY] (Complaint No. [NUMBER])
  •  [THE JOINT COMMISSION / CARF] (Incident No. [NUMBER])
  •  [STATE] Attorney General, Consumer Protection Division (No. [NUMBER])
  •  [STATE] Department of Insurance (No. [NUMBER])

Each complaint includes copies of my correspondence and the certified mail
return receipts establishing your facility's receipt and non-response.

My requests are unchanged: the complete itemized statement, the complete medical
record, and written confirmation that this disputed account is on hold and has
not been referred to collections or reported to any consumer reporting agency.

I remain willing to resolve this directly and will notify each agency promptly
upon satisfactory resolution.

Sincerely,

_______________________________
[YOUR FULL LEGAL NAME]
```
