# 03 — Final Demand Before Filing Complaints (Send ~Day 31)

> **Send by:** Certified Mail w/ Return Receipt to the facility **and** to the
> corporate registered agent + fax + email + portal.
>
> **Purpose:** Last chance before external escalation. File the complaints in
> [06-regulatory-complaints.md](06-regulatory-complaints.md) the same week — do not
> send this and then wait indefinitely. A deadline you don't enforce is worthless.
